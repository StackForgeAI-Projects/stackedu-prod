# StackEDU Bursar User Guide

## Who this is for

Bursars manage student fees, payments, receipts, and financial reporting at your institution.

## Quick walkthrough — what each page does

### Dashboard
See today’s collections, outstanding balances, fee holds, and recent payments. Use quick links to receipts, reports, or reconciliation.

### Payment Ledger
Search every payment by method, status, or date. Open a row to see transaction details and download a receipt when one exists.

### Fee Structure
Add tuition and levy items by programme and year group. When ready, click **Apply to semester** so matching students receive invoices on their **Fees** page.

### Student Accounts
Browse all students with balances and holds. Open a student to record a manual payment (bank transfer, cash) or place/release a fee hold.

### Receipts
Find issued receipts, filter by date, and void incorrect receipts with a reason (recorded in the audit log).

### Fee Messages
Send fee reminders to:
- All enrolled students
- Students in selected departments or years
- Students enrolled in specific courses
- Individual students

Students receive these as in-app notifications linked to their Fees page.

### Reports
Generate live summaries for today, this month, the current semester, or the year — with collection trends and programme breakdown.

### Reconciliation
Work through gateway transactions that are pending or mismatched. Match them to students or mark them resolved.

### Notifications
Your in-app inbox for fee alerts, reconciliation items, and system messages. Click the bell icon in the header.

## How to set fees for a semester

1. Go to **Fee Structure**.
2. Click **Add fee item** — enter name, category, amount, programme, and year group.
3. Click **Apply to semester** when the structure is ready.
4. Students who match the programme/year rules receive a semester invoice on **Student → Fees**.

**Note:** Students only see fees after invoices are issued. Adding fee items alone does not charge students until you apply them to the semester (or they pay against an existing invoice).
