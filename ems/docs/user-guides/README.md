# StackEDU user guides

These are the role guides for StackEDU. Open the guide that matches your account and follow it from top to bottom. You do not need any other document.

Each guide stands on its own: who it is for, how to sign in, what is in the menu, and how to complete the usual work for that role.

## How to open the app

Use the StackEDU app URL your institution sent you.

- New students start with **Apply** on the sign-in page.
- Everyone else uses **Sign in**.
- You do not pick a role. The account decides who you are.

Sign in with the email (or student number) your institution gave you. Do not share passwords.

## Rules that apply in every guide

1. Open the app on a phone, tablet, or computer. The layout stacks on a small screen.
2. Use sample details where a form asks for them, unless you mean to save real personal data.
3. Dates on screen are **DD-MM-YYYY**.
4. Create, update, and delete always ask you to confirm before they save.
5. If a step is not in a guide, do not assume it works.

> [!NOTE]
> **Tip** boxes are extra help. **Watch** boxes are easy to miss, or they change live data.
