# Academic Admin guide

This guide walks you through the registry work in StackEDU: applications, students, courses, timetable, results, and reports.

**Who this is for:** You work in the registry.

**How to start:** Open the StackEDU app URL your institution sent you. Sign in with your Academic Admin email. The app takes you to the Academic Admin dashboard. Use the steps below on live admissions, courses, and results.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates on screen use **DD-MM-YYYY**. When you create, update, or delete something, the app asks you to confirm before it saves.

## Sign in

1. Open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

The sidebar shows: Dashboard, Applications, Students, Courses, Programmes, Calendar, Timetable, Faculty, Results, Reports, At-Risk, E-Library, Notifications.

---

## Dashboard

You see a greeting and today’s date as weekday plus **DD-MM-YYYY**.

Tiles show live counts:

- **Total enrolled**
- **Pending applications**
- **Results pending approval**
- **At-risk students**

**Recent Applications** lets you **Review** a file. **Result Approval Queue** lists lecturer batches that still need you. Upcoming calendar events use **DD-MM-YYYY**.

---

## Applications

Open **Applications**. Search by name or application ID. Open a row to see personal details, school history, programme, documents, and fee status. Dates use **DD-MM-YYYY**.

### Mark under review

1. Leave **Mark under review** selected (or pick Accept / Reject / Request documents).
2. Click **Save decision**.
3. Confirm **Mark as under review?** / **Confirm and notify student**.

The file moves to **UNDER REVIEW**. The applicant sees the update on Track.

> [!WARNING]
> **Accept** creates a student offer. Only accept if you mean to.

---

## Students

Open **Students**. Search and filters work. Open a student to see their file. Dates that exist show as **DD-MM-YYYY**.

---

## Courses

Open **Courses**. The catalogue lists courses, registration open or closed, and the assigned lecturer. You can **Add course**.

---

## Programmes

Open **Programmes**. You see programmes and how many students are enrolled. You can **Add programme**.

---

## Calendar

Open **Calendar**. Event dates use **DD-MM-YYYY**. You can **Add event** or **Publish calendar**.

---

## Timetable

Open **Timetable**. This is a **review** grid. Lecturers add slots. A clash box lists booked rooms and when a lecturer is already teaching. Open a class and **message the lecturer** if you need that hall. You cannot double-book a taken room.

---

## Results

Open **Results**. **Pending Approval** is the queue. **Published** is what students can already see.

### Review a batch

1. Click **Review & Approve** on a pending course.
2. Check the average, highest, lowest, and pass rate.
3. Click **Approve and publish**.
4. Confirm **Publish these results?**

The batch leaves Pending and appears on **Published**. Students can then see those grades.

---

## Faculty

Open **Faculty**. You see lecturers created by ICT. Search and the department filter work.

Click **View profile** to see department, email, and assigned courses.

Assign a lecturer to a course from the **Courses** page, not from this list.

---

## Reports

Open **Reports**. Choose a report, then **Generate report**.

You can generate enrollment, results, attendance, and programme summaries from live numbers.

---

## At-Risk

Open **At-Risk**. Filters: All / High / Medium / Low, plus programme.

Click a student name to open their file.

---

## E-Library

Open **E-Library**. Search the published catalogue. Filter by type.

### Request a title

1. Click **Request a title**.
2. Enter a title, author, and why you need it.
3. Click **Send request**, then confirm.

The librarian sees the request.

---

## Notifications

Open **Notifications**. Filters include All, Unread, Applications, Results, Registration, At-Risk.

Dates use **DD-MM-YYYY**. Click a row to open it. Opening an unread item marks it read. The bell count drops.

---

## If the app signs you out

If you sit idle too long, the app signs you out. Sign in again with the same email and password.
