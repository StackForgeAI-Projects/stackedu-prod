# Applicant guide

This guide walks you through applying for admission in StackEDU, from creating an account to tracking your file.

**Who this is for:** You want to apply. You do not have a student login yet.

**How to start:** Open the StackEDU app URL your institution sent you. On the sign-in page, choose **Apply**. Follow the sections below in order.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates on screen use **DD-MM-YYYY**. When you create, update, or delete something, the app asks you to confirm before it saves.

## Create your account

1. Open **Apply**.
2. Fill in:
   - Full name
   - Email
   - Phone (the form may already show a country code — type the rest of the number)
   - Programme of interest
   - Password, then the same password again
3. Click **Create account**.

> [!TIP]
> The password must be at least 8 characters and include a letter and a number. If the two password boxes do not match, the button stays off.

---

## Verify your email

A box opens: **Verify your account**. It asks for a 6-digit code sent to your email. The code lasts **15 minutes**. You can ask for a new code after 30 seconds.

1. Type the 6 digits one box at a time.
2. Click **Verify email**.
3. You land on the application form. Your application ID is in the side panel.

> [!WARNING]
> Use the code from your email inbox. If mail is not set up, the people running the app can tell you where to read the code instead.

---

## Fill the application form

The form has seven steps. Dates use the date picker. Click **Continue** after each step. The app saves as you go.

### Personal details

Fill name, date of birth (you must be at least 16), gender, nationality, ID type, national ID or passport number, phone, country, **district**, **sector**, and physical address.

> [!TIP]
> Sector stays locked until you enter a district.

### Academic history

Enter school name, country, highest qualification, exam index number, year of completion, aggregate, and principal subjects.

If you pick **A-Level** (or similar), you must also pick a subject combination.

### Programme

Confirm the programme, pick the **entry year / intake**, and leave study mode as **Full-time** if that is what you want.

### Parent / guardian

Pick guardian type, then name, relationship, phone, and occupation. Email and employer are optional.

### Additional info

Write a personal statement of **at least 50 words**. Add an emergency contact (name, phone, relationship). Tick the declaration that your answers are true. Then click **Continue to documents**.

## Upload documents

Open **Documents**. Accepted files are PDF, JPG, and PNG. Upload every file marked required. Typical required files are:

- National ID or Passport
- School leaving certificate
- Academic transcripts / results slip
- Passport-size photo
- Medical insurance proof

Optional files can wait. **Continue to payment** stays off until every required file is uploaded. You can **Remove** a file and upload again.

You can drag and drop or click to browse. The counter shows how many required documents are in.

---

## Pay the fee and submit

On **Application fee** you see the amount to pay.

1. Choose a payment method (mobile money, card, or bank transfer).
2. Enter the details the form asks for.
3. Click the pay-and-submit button and wait for confirmation.

When it works, you see **Application Submitted!** and your application ID. You can copy the ID.

> [!TIP]
> Bank transfer shows the institution’s bank details and your application ID as the reference. Mobile money or card may complete faster, depending on how payments are set up.

---

## Track your application

Click **Track my application**.

You should see:

- Your name, programme, and application ID
- Dates as **DD-MM-YYYY** for started, fee paid, and sent
- The current status (for example **Sent for review**)
- A short message about what happens next

Your initials in the top bar open:

- **Personal Information** — name, ID, email, phone (read only)
- **Change Password**
- **Log out** — a confirm box asks **Log out?** before the session ends

Already applied? From the create-account page use **Track your application**. Sign in with your email **or** your application ID and the same password. You can also sign in from the main **Sign in** page.

---

## What you cannot do yet

You cannot register for courses from this account. Academic Admin reviews the application first. After they accept you and you take the offer, you sign in as a student.
