# ICT Manager guide

<span style="background:#DCFCE7;color:#16A34A;padding:3px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-weight:700;">Works</span> Use the steps below on the live institution.

## Sign in

1. Open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

The sidebar shows: Dashboard, User Management, Access Levels, Access Revocation, Audit Log, System Settings, Integrations, Analytics, Notifications, Announcements.

## Dashboard

You see a greeting, today’s date as weekday plus **DD-MM-YYYY**, and the institution name.

Tiles: **Platform users**, **Active accounts**, **Open sessions**, **Active revocations**.

**Users by role** and **Recent audit** load from live data. Search the audit table. Click **View** on a row (dismiss the on-page guide first if it sits on top of the buttons).

## User Management

Open **User Management**. Search by name, email, or student number. Filter by role and status.

**Add user** creates a real login. A student account also creates their student record so they can open the student portal.

<strong style="color:#DC2626;">Watch</strong><br/>
Do not revoke or disable accounts other people still need.
</div>

## Access Levels

Open **Access Levels**. Pick a role (Academic Admin, Applicant, Bursar, ICT Manager, Lecturer, Librarian, Student). Each switch is one action.

The timetable switch explains that lecturers set times and a room cannot be booked twice.

Do not save changes unless you mean to change what that role can do.

## Access Revocation

Open **Access Revocation**. Search and filters load even if the list is empty. Restore is on the row when a revocation exists.

## Audit Log

Open **Audit Log**. Search action, actor, or target. Filter by action.

Click **View** to open one record. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.

## System Settings

Open **System Settings**. You can change:

- Institution name and short name
- Contact email and website
- Logo and system colour
- **Timezone** (class times and reminder emails use this)
- Language
- Attendance edit window
- Payment collection (full payment or instalments)

Click **Save settings** only if you changed something and confirmed.

## Integrations

Open **Integrations**. Tabs: All, Payments, Email, SMS, Classroom, AI.

Open a service to add keys, turn it on, and check the connection. **Resend Email** sends admissions, account, timetable reminder, and notification emails. Mail is skipped when that service is off.

Do not paste production keys unless you intend to send live mail or take live payments.

## Analytics

Open **Analytics**. Tiles include total users, active sessions, pending revocations, logins, new users, audit events, and services on. **Accounts by role** and **Activity snapshot** load from live counts.

## Notifications

Open **Notifications**. System messages sent to this ICT account appear here. The page can be empty. That is normal.

## Announcements

Open **Announcements**. Compose a title and message. **Everyone** sends to all active logins. **Selected groups** lets you narrow the audience. Recipients get an in-app notice, not SMS or email.

You can also **View** an announcement that is already published.
