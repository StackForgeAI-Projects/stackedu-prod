# Lecturer guide

This guide walks you through the lecturer portal in StackEDU: courses, classroom, timetable, attendance, results, and assignments.

**Who this is for:** You teach courses.

**How to start:** Open the StackEDU app URL your institution sent you. Sign in with your lecturer email. The app takes you to the lecturer dashboard — you do not pick a role. Use the steps below on the courses assigned to you.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates on screen use **DD-MM-YYYY**. When you create, update, or delete something, the app asks you to confirm before it saves.

## Sign in

1. Open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

The sidebar shows: Dashboard, My Courses, Course Management, Classroom, E-Library, Timetable, Attendance, Results, Assignments, Assessment Builder, Analytics, Notifications.

---

## Dashboard

You see a greeting and today’s date as weekday plus **DD-MM-YYYY**.

Tiles show your live record:

- **Assigned courses**
- **Pending result entries**
- **At-risk students** (if any)

Course cards list the classes you teach. Each card can have **Enter results**. If you have no class today, the schedule can say **No classes today**.

---

## My Courses

Open **My Courses**. The left list has your assigned courses. Click a course name to open it.

Use the tabs for students, attendance, and course content. Use **Add material**, **Course Management**, or **Create assignment** when you need them.

**Add material** needs at least one module on that course. If there is none, hover **Add material** (or the info icon) and use the link to open **Add module** in Course Management. After you create a module, you can add a page, file, or link and attach it to that module. Files must be 14 MB or less.

---

## Course Management

Pick a course in the list at the top.

You should see modules, pages, announcements, live classes, and discussions for that course.

### Add a module

A module is a named part of the course. It could be Week 1, Week 2, and so on. It is a simple way to break learning into smaller bits for your students. You add pages, files, and other course content inside it. Students open that content by module. If you set a prerequisite, they cannot open this module until they finish the earlier one.

1. Click **Add module**.
2. Enter a title. A summary and prerequisite are optional.
3. Confirm **Add this module?**

You need at least one module before you can add course material. The info box on the form explains this in the same words.

### Post an announcement

1. Click **Post announcement**.
2. Enter a title and a short message.
3. Click **Post**, then confirm **Post this announcement?**

The announcement appears in the list. Students see it on that course page.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not delete live modules or pages unless you mean to.

## Classroom

Open **Classroom**. Pick a course and schedule a class, or open an existing session. Live video needs classroom keys to be set by ICT.

**Automatically record live classes** sits at the top. Confirm to turn it on or off. When it is on, starting a class also starts a recording if storage is set.

On a **Live** class:

1. **Join as host** (or **Start class** joins you).
2. A red **Recording** mark shows on the video while a recording is running.
3. Confirm **Mute all** to silence every student microphone. You stay unmuted.
4. **Full screen** expands the video only. The name stays on the button in dark ink. The same is true in picture-in-picture.
5. The red **Leave** control drops you from the room. It does not open picture-in-picture.
6. Opening another sidebar page while you are still in the class keeps the call in a small picture-in-picture window. **Back to class** returns. **Leave** on that window drops you from the room. **End class** closes it for everyone.
7. Draw on the whiteboard. Students see the same marks while the class is live.

After you confirm **End class**, nobody can draw on the board. Students get a notice to download a ready recording before the keep-window ICT set (default 72 hours).

## E-Library

Open **E-Library**. Search published titles. You can click **Request a title** if a book is missing.

---

## Timetable

Open **Timetable**. Academic Admin sets the times. Open a class and **Confirm availability**, or **Request a change** and write why. Students see the class only after you confirm. A red box lists a room or time that overlaps. Ask Academic Admin to move it.

---

## Attendance

Open **Attendance**. Pick a course. History dates are **DD-MM-YYYY**.

1. Click **Start session**.
2. Mark each student **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**.
3. Click **Submit attendance** and confirm.

Submitted rows stay in history. After the ICT edit window, they are no longer editable.

---

## Results

Open **Results**. Pick a course. Enter marks. Grades appear as you type. The page shows average, highest, and lowest.

1. Click **Save draft** and confirm. The batch stays a draft.
2. When the row is complete, click **Submit for publishing** and confirm **Submit results for publishing?**

Students do **not** see these marks yet. Academic Admin reviews them first. After submit, the batch shows as waiting for review.

---

## Assignments

Open **Assignments**. Pick a task, then **View submissions**.

1. Open a student who has submitted.
2. Enter a score and short feedback.
3. Click **Save grade** and confirm.

---

## Assessment Builder

Open **Assessment Builder**. You can **Create Assessment/Quiz**, **Build questions**, or **Add question**. Do not delete live questions unless you mean to.

---

## Analytics

Open **Analytics**. Pick a course. Tiles show attendance, materials opened, average score, and at-risk. The student table lists people on that course.

---

## Notifications

Open the bell in the header, or open **Notifications** in the sidebar. Dates on the list are **DD-MM-YYYY**. Click an unread item in the dropdown or on the page. It marks as read at once and the red number on the bell drops. **View all notifications** opens the full list.

---

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** in the sidebar and confirm.
