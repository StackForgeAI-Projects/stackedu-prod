# Student guide

<span style="background:#DCFCE7;color:#16A34A;padding:3px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-weight:700;">Works</span> Use the steps below on your own record.

## Sign in

1. Open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email **or** student number, then your password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

You land on **Dashboard**. The sidebar shows: Dashboard, My Courses, Register, Classroom, Timetable, Results, Fees, E-Library, Notifications.

## Dashboard

You see a greeting, your student ID, and today’s date as weekday plus **DD-MM-YYYY**.

Tiles show your live record:

- **Current GPA** — matches published results
- **Outstanding fees** — **View statement** if nothing is due, or **Pay now** if a balance remains
- **Attendance rate** — across recorded sessions

Below that: your registered courses, recent results, a month calendar, today’s schedule, and upcoming deadlines.

<span style="background:#DBEAFE;color:#2563EB;padding:3px 10px;border-radius:999px;font-weight:700;">Tip</span> Courses, fees, results, and timetable come from your real record, even if a helper box on the dashboard is paused.

## My Courses

Open **My Courses**. You see the current semester and the courses you registered.

Click a course. You get:

- Announcements
- Live classes
- Study assistant
- Discussions
- Attendance
- Course content in modules (a later module can stay locked until the one before is finished)
- Assignments with due dates as **DD-MM-YYYY**

Click **Read page** on a reading to open it. Click **Submit** on an assignment to go to the submit screen.

## Submit an assignment

From the course page, click **Submit** on a written assignment.

1. Write your answer in **Your response**.
2. Click **Submit assignment**.
3. Confirm **Submit this assignment?** The box says you cannot edit after this.

When it works, the page says the assignment is already submitted. The lecturer marks it from Assignments.

## Register

Open **Register**. You see how many courses and credits you already have, and whether registration is open.

What you can do:

- Tick a course to add it. If it would go over the credit cap, a **Credit limit** box appears and **Confirm registration** stays off.
- Click **Drop** on a registered course, then confirm (or **Cancel** if you only wanted to see the box).
- Click **Download PDF** to download your current registration list (this does not change anything).

## Classroom

Open **Classroom**. Pick a live class and click **Open**.

The session page opens. If video is not set up yet, you may see a message that the whiteboard still works, or that the class has not started. Wait until the lecturer starts the class.

## Timetable

Open **Timetable**. You get a Monday–Friday grid of your classes (hall, lab, time).

1. Click a class.
2. A reminder box opens (title, when to send, optional notes).
3. Add a note if you want. Click **Save reminder**.
4. Confirm **Save this reminder?**

The app can email you a calendar file and put a notice in **Notifications**. After save, the bell can show an unread item.

## Results

Open **Results**. You see CGPA, standing, and semester GPA. Search and type filters sit above the table. Only **published** results appear here.

Click **Transcript →**. You get an unofficial transcript: name, student number, programme, CGPA, and the semester block. The page says the official copy is issued by Academic Admin. Use **← Results** to go back.

## Fees

Open **Fees**. You see **Charged / Paid / Balance**, then invoice and payment tables you can search.

If the balance is **0**, there may be nothing to pay. Paying an invoice needs a charge on your account first.

## E-Library

Open **E-Library**. The catalogue lists published titles.

- Search by title, author, or subject.
- Click **Request a title**, enter a title (and why you need it), click **Send request**, then confirm.

The librarian later sees that request.

## Notifications

Open **Notifications**. You see reminders, assignments, fees, registration, and results. Each row has **Open →**. Tap an unread item to mark it read.
